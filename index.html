Why women choose CFS

Done working hard for results that never show up?

Hours of cardio. Bootcamps that leave you sore and stuck. Diets that worked until they didn't. Many of our clients came to us right there. What changed wasn't effort. It was coaching.

Strength first

We're a weight lifting and strength-focused studio, built for the aging athlete, the busy mom or dad and the business professional. Smarter workouts, not more high-impact ones.

Coached every session

Never more than 8 clients per coach, so you get real coaching and form corrections every time you train.

Food without the rules

Nutrition coaching that teaches you to fuel your body without cutting carbs, eliminating food groups you love or drastically slashing calories.

Men train here too, and they're welcome. Every client gets the same thing: a coach who knows your goals and a plan built only for you.

FAQ

Ask us anything

Still wondering if CFS is right for you? Call (608) 630-8324 and ask.

I'm new to fitness training. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Our programs are tailored to all fitness levels. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or just beginning, our trainers give you the guidance, support and motivation you need, in an inclusive, supportive environment where everyone feels welcome.

What programs do you offer?

Personal Training, Small Group Training, Nutrition Coaching and Contest Prep. Each one is designed around your needs, so you get the right training to reach your goals.

How much is small group training?

12 sessions a month is $199, unlimited sessions are $259 a month, and a 10 pack is $200 to use over 12 months. No long-term contracts or commitments.

Where are you located?

Our gym is at 6723 Seybold Rd, Madison, WI 53719, and our office is at 802B Lois Drive, Sun Prairie, WI 53590.
